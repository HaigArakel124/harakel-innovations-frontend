Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HARAKEL INNOVATIONS LLC ("Harakel Innovations", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit harakel-innovations.com, sign up for or use our software, or receive text messages from us.

1. Information we collect

When you sign up. Your business name and address, industry, your name, email address and business phone, the plan you choose, and, if you add them, your mobile number, website, business hours and services.

Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe's secure checkout page; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded and the status of your subscription.

Your mobile number and text-message consent. Your mobile number is optional. If you tick the text-message box at signup, we collect your mobile number and a record of your consent: the time, the version of the consent wording you agreed to, and the page where you gave it.

When you use the service. Your sign-in details (passwords are stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text), the settings you choose, and the information you add, such as your services, hours and appointments.

Information we process for salons. Conversations with a salon's website chatbot, calls answered by its AI phone line (call details and transcripts), text messages sent and received and emails sent through the service, and appointments and leads. Section 5 explains how we handle it.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting providers record technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We also keep records of the IP addresses and email addresses used with some features, such as sign-in, to block abuse and spam. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals do not change how our site works. Our client portal stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in.

2. How we use information

  • To set up, run and support your account and the features in your plan.
  • To send account emails, such as your welcome email and password resets.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To power the AI features for your business: the chatbot, the AI phone line, text replies and the business coach.
  • If you add your mobile number, to forward calls from your business line to you when your call settings ask for it.
  • To send the text messages you asked for (section 3).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information, or your customers' information, for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. Text messages (SMS)

We text your mobile number only to send the account notifications you asked for (signup confirmation, setup and service alerts), and to honour HELP and STOP requests.

Giving consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. If you do not tick the text-message box, we never text your mobile number.

Harakel Innovations will never sell, rent or share your mobile number, or your text-message opt-in data and consent, with any third party or affiliate for their marketing or promotional purposes. We give your number only to the service providers that store it for us or deliver our texts and calls, and only so they can do that.

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help, START to resubscribe. You can also email support@harakel-innovations.com.

The full terms of our text-message program are in our Terms of Service.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only with the service providers that help us run Harakel Innovations, only as much as each one needs, and only so it can provide its service to us:

  • Supabase: our database, which stores account and service information.
  • Railway: hosts our application servers.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • SendGrid: sends our emails.
  • Twilio: phone numbers, calls and text messages, including turning callers' speech into text.
  • Anthropic: the AI model behind the chatbot, the AI phone line, text replies and the business coach.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load their fonts from Google, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

None of this sharing is for anyone's marketing. Your mobile number and your text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes (section 3).

5. Data about a salon's own customers

Salons and med spas use Harakel Innovations to talk with their own customers by chat, phone, text and email. We process that information (such as names, phone numbers, messages and appointment details) on the salon's behalf and under its instructions. Texts that a salon sends to its own customers are governed by that salon's own policies, not by our text-message program. If you are a customer of a salon, please contact the salon about your information; we will help the salon respond.

6. Security

All traffic to our website and servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash, card details are handled only by Stripe, and access to personal information is limited to the people and systems that need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and service information: while your account is open, and afterwards for as long as we need it for legal, tax, accounting or dispute reasons (for example, billing records). You can ask us to delete it sooner (section 8).
  • Text-message consent records: five years from the date we record them, even if you later opt out or close your account, as evidence of your consent.
  • Opt-out (STOP) records: as long as needed to make sure we never text you after you opt out.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can stop our texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop a particular use of it, by emailing support@harakel-innovations.com. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep information we need for legal, accounting or evidence reasons, such as billing and consent records.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other privacy rights under local law. We will honour them as that law requires.

9. Children

Harakel Innovations is a service for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website or signup. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell our customers by email before it takes effect.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: email support@harakel-innovations.com.
